Abstract

This interactive narrative explores three approaches to completing a college assignmen, using AI as a shortcut, using AI as a study tool,and working independently. Each decision leads to a different outcome, demonstrating how technology can influence learning and understanding. The story encourages students to think critically about using AI responsibly while developing their own knowledge.
